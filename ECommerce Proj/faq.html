Common Questions


What services do you offer? (click here)

We offer a range of services including Computer SI (System Integrator) support, Behavioral Science support, and History topics support. Each service is designed to meet specific needs of our customers:

  • Computer SI support: We assist with system integration, hardware and software compatibility, network configuration, and IT infrastructure optimization.
  • Behavioral Science support: Our experts provide guidance on research methodologies, data analysis, and interpretation of social and behavioral studies across various domains like psychology, sociology, and anthropology.
  • History topics support: We offer resources and expertise on historical research, document analysis, and contextual interpretation across different periods and geographical regions.

How can I get started with your services? (click here)

Getting started is straightforward! Here's a step-by-step guide:

  1. Sign Up: Create an account on our website by providing basic information and selecting the service(s) you're interested in (Computer SI support, Social and Behavioral Science support, or History topics support).
  2. Choose Your Package: Select from our tiered service packages tailored to your needs. Each package includes specific benefits like priority support, access to exclusive resources, or personalized consultations.
  3. Onboarding Process: After signing up, you'll receive a welcome email with detailed onboarding instructions. This includes links to our knowledge base, introductory webinars, and contact information for your dedicated support team.
  4. Initial Consultation: Schedule an initial consultation with our experts to discuss your specific requirements and goals. This ensures we tailor our services to meet your unique needs.
